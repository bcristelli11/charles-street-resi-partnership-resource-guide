Partnership executive summary

Recommendation

Adopt Resi as Charles Street's centralized website, SEO, and digital advertising platform. A custom Enterprise Theme replaces the fragmented vendor model, fixes the attribution gaps, builds the organic foundation to reduce paid dependency, and scales cleanly across the nine additional properties in the pipeline.

The challenge

Charles Street runs six core Midwest properties across a fragmented stack: Agency 53 on websites, Conversion Logix on paid advertising, and separate content vendors. The model has produced heavy ad spend with minimal return, attribution gaps that limit reporting to leadership, and no organic engine to offset paid media. With Cardinal coming in-house this spring and nine more properties in the pipeline, the need for a single platform that scales with the portfolio is immediate.

What Resi solves

Custom website differentiation at portfolio scale

A Premium Enterprise Theme is a ground-up custom framework built for the Charles Street brand, not a modified template. The theme is developed once over 8-10 weeks, then rolled out across the six properties in 3-5 days per site. Every property gets layout-level customization, custom components, and a cohesive design language. Each additional property added through the partnership rolls out on the same framework in days, not weeks.

Organic-first strategy to reduce paid dependency

SEO is the growth lever you called out in our March conversation. Premium SEO includes monthly evergreen content, keyword strategy per property, quarterly business reviews, live pricing pushed to Google Business Profile, and content built for generative search. Case study benchmark: a recent Resi client saw 480% organic traffic growth and doubled conversion rates within a single quarter. Every dollar of organic traffic is a dollar that doesn't need to come from Google Ads.

A platform Cardinal and Charles Street can scale into

As Cardinal takes over in-house this spring, the team gets direct platform access on day one: one login for all properties plus the corporate site, a centralized CMS with global content, a digital asset vault, and integrations management built in. As the nine additional properties come online, each rollout happens in-platform without an agency middle layer.

Attribution that holds up with equity partners

Native attribution normalizes lead sources across every form on every site (UTM, URL referral, ID). Source persists from first click through to the Entrata submission, so you're reporting on real marketing ROI by property and channel, not a black-box "website" source. GTM and GA4 are configured at launch. That's the data layer that makes the equity partner conversation easier, not harder.

Questions and answers

Pulled from our conversations with you and the Charles Street team.

Website and platform

Why Enterprise Theme over Resi-Made for Charles Street?

Enterprise is a ground-up custom framework built for Charles Street's brand, not a modified template. Layout-level customization, custom components, and a cohesive design language across all properties. Post-launch, your team has full builder access with 500+ layouts and 92+ elements to build landing pages, swap sections, or spin up microsites without dev work. Once built, each property rollout takes 3-5 days, and every future acquisition rolls out on the same framework. Resi-Made Themes are strong for faster launches (Winona, the theme you liked, is in that library), but Enterprise is the right fit for a growing portfolio that needs brand consistency.

Does Resi integrate with Entrata? What if the CRM changes later?

Entrata is a strong integration for us with established patterns across existing clients. You'll have full visibility into sync logs and control over sync frequency. Beyond Entrata, Resi supports 42+ native integrations across PMS, CRM, ILS, GBP, and analytics tools, so the integrations hub lets you connect, test, and swap tools without rebuilding sites. If the CRM changes, we reconnect the new system on the backend and the sites keep running.

How does the rollout align with Cardinal coming in-house this spring?

It aligns well. Discovery (2 weeks) and Enterprise Theme development (8-10 weeks) run while Cardinal is standing up operations. Sites go live in the final 4 weeks across two rollout phases. Cardinal's team gets direct platform access on launch day: one login, centralized CMS, and integrations management built in. No agency middle layer to slow them down.

When the next property comes online, what's the turnaround?

On the Enterprise Theme: 3-5 business days per site post-asset collection. Asset collection typically takes 1-2 days, and our CX team pre-fills the onboarding form by scraping existing sites and flagging content gaps. The $1,650 discounted rollout rate is locked for additional properties added through September 30, 2026.

SEO strategy

What does month 1 through 6 of SEO actually look like?

Month 1-2: technical foundation. Audits, schema, GBP optimization across all properties, keyword strategy per property. Month 3-4: content engine goes live. Monthly evergreen content (neighborhood pages, local guides, amenity-focused pages) with content sequenced to leasing cycles. Month 5-6: data feedback loop. First QBR, content performance review, organic traffic lift measurable.

How do SEO and paid ads work together over time?

The SEO and ads teams share data and sit under the same roof. Paid keyword performance informs SEO priorities. Organic ranking improvements let us pull back paid on terms where we're winning. Landing pages for paid campaigns live on the property domain, so ad traffic builds site authority. Long term, paid shifts from foundational to supplemental, targeted at lease-up moments or underperforming properties.

Digital advertising

Why would Resi's ad management perform differently than what we've seen?

Three things. Ads run on fully managed websites with GTM and GA4 configured at launch, so tracking works from day one. Budget recommendations are driven by occupancy data pulled from Entrata, not spend targets. And optimization, reporting, and QBRs are handled by the same team that runs the campaigns.

How does the 20% management fee structure work?

20% of monthly media spend (excludes SEO). When per-property spend exceeds ~$5,625/mo, the 20% calculation governs. Below that, the Pro tier $1,125/mo minimum applies. For Charles Street's scenarios: Conservative ($6,000) → $1,200, Balanced ($9,000) → $1,800, Aggressive ($14,000) → $2,800.

How does Resi fix the attribution issues?

Native attribution normalizes lead sources (UTM, URL referral, ID) across every form. Source persists from first click through to the Entrata submission. You'll see the specific channel and campaign driving each lead, not a black-box "website" source. GTM and GA4 are configured at launch; sync logs are visible in the platform. DNI-based call tracking can be layered in.

Can we adjust spend month to month based on occupancy?

Yes. Decreases take effect within 72 hours. Increases depend on funding timing. We recommend reallocating rather than maintaining maintenance budgets on stabilized properties. The goal is spend efficiency, not spend volume.

Reporting and support

What does reporting look like?

Real-time dashboards for website, SEO, and PPC metrics accessible through the platform. GA4 integrated at launch. Monthly performance summaries and quarterly business reviews covering website performance, SEO (rankings, organic traffic, content, GBP), and ads (campaign performance, cost per lead, budget efficiency). Built for straightforward equity partner conversations. Sample QBRs linked in Resources.

Who manages the account day to day?

A dedicated CX director owns the relationship through onboarding and ongoing. SEO and digital ads teams work alongside CX, all under one roof. Revyse 2025 recognized Resi as Support Star, Most Loved, and Best Value. Ticket turnaround is 24-48 hours, with emergency protocols for critical issues outside business hours. One point of contact, one platform, no handoffs between website, SEO, and ads.
