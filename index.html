04

Questions the team will ask

Partner access and ownership

How does Adawk get access?

We invite Adawk as a client admin, so they work in the CMS alongside your team with no ticket queue in between. You grant and revoke that access, so if the agency relationship changes, the access changes with it.

Who owns the Google properties and ad accounts?

Charles Street does, from day one. Google Business Profile, Analytics, Ads, and Search Console are client-owned, and any listing or ad account Adawk builds belongs to you, not to Resi or the agency.

What about Tag Manager and hosting credentials?

Tag Manager runs through a parallel container, so Adawk manages their own tags without touching the one that runs the site. Hosting and SFTP credentials aren't issued to third parties. That's a platform-wide security standard, and it's why we can stand behind uptime and backups.

Platform and build

Are the sites WordPress? Who hosts them?

No WordPress. Every site is built in Next.js, and Resi hosts and manages it, which is what lets us stand behind uptime, security, and the Entrata integration. Your domains, Google properties, and content stay yours.

Does Resi work with Entrata and Elise, and what if our CRM changes?

Both are established integrations for us. If the CRM changes mid-term, we reconnect it on the backend and the sites keep running, with nothing rebuilt. We confirm your live connections property by property during discovery.

How do you handle Sawyer & Huck's two buildings?

As one site with segmented availability, so a renter shopping one building sees that building's floor plans and pricing without leaving the site. It stays one domain and one CMS entry, with the second property added at the shared-site rate of $94.05 a month.

Can our SightMap work with the floor plans instead of separately?

Yes. For properties with Engrain, the SightMap is built into the shopping experience, so units on the map connect to the same units, pricing, and tour options in the list.

Do we still need a separate tour scheduling tool?

If a tool only handles tour scheduling today, likely not. Resi's native scheduler books in-person, virtual, and self-guided tours directly into Entrata. If the tool also handles other work, like lead follow-up, we'll map that in discovery and keep it connected where it makes sense.

What can our team change, and what goes through Resi?

Your team updates copy, photos, and specials in the platform, or submits a ticket and our team does it. New pages and sections go through a support ticket to our support and web design teams. Each property's 6 design hours a year are for net new design work, like a new landing page or campaign.

How does adding a new property work?

It rolls out on the existing framework at your locked $2,337.50, with no new theme to build. Our CX team pre-fills the onboarding form from your existing site and flags any content gaps, so there's little for your team to gather.

What are your support response times?

24 to 48 hours on standard tickets, with emergency protocols outside business hours. Anything touching pricing, availability, or lead generation goes to the top of the queue automatically.

Data and tracking

How do we know lead tracking is actually working this time?

You can check it before you depend on it. Resi Pixel's tester shows how a given URL, referrer, or click ID will resolve, and the value written into your lead source field, per property before launch. After launch, a log records the evidence behind every visit, so any number in a report can be traced back.

What does Resi track, and can Adawk use it?

Every key renter action is recorded and analyzed by Resi with no setup. The same events are available to Adawk in GA4, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads, and Meta, including booked tours, Apply clicks, calls, and form submissions as conversions, each counted once.

Is our renter data private?

Yes. Every site ships with a consent banner and Google Consent Mode v2. Names, emails, and phone numbers never appear in any analytics event.

Are the sites built for AI search?

Every site ships search-ready, with schema markup, structured data, meta titles and descriptions, image alt text, and clean page structure. The ongoing content and keyword work on top of that sits with Adawk.

Switching and investment

How do we avoid losing leads when we switch?

Each site is built and tested on a staging URL while your current site stays live. Entrata sync, form routing, and attribution are verified before cutover, and 301 redirects carry existing URLs over so search equity and ad links don't break. The domain moves only after everything passes.

What's the simplest way to frame the investment?

One fixed number and two recurring ones. The build is $30,112.50 for all seven sites. Each future property is $2,337.50, and management is $379.05 per site per month.

Why did the build price come down?

Building in Next.js takes our team less time. Our team recently built 10 sites from scratch in 6 hours. The theme development discount moved to 50%, and we passed that efficiency straight through.

Is SEO or digital advertising part of this?

No. This proposal covers websites and platform management only, since Adawk holds SEO and paid media. Both are available from Resi if that changes, scoped separately.

What happens at the end of the term?

Rates are tied to a 3-year agreement. Your rollout rate holds for every property added during the term, so growth doesn't require renegotiating each time a property comes online.
