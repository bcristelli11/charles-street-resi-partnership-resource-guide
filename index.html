01

The opportunity

Recommendation

Move Charles Street's seven property websites and the corporate site onto Resi, built on one custom Enterprise Theme. This is a websites-only engagement. Your SEO and paid partner works inside the platform with the access they need, your team owns every property and ad account from day one, and every property you add later rolls out on a framework that is already built and paid for.

Why now

Charles Street is exiting its current website host and needs a new home for seven property sites plus corporate. The sites need a rebuild, not a migration. Your consultant's review made that clear, and it is also why your team ranked Resi's websites first on their evaluation.

The brief has narrowed since the spring. SEO and paid media now sit with Adawk, so this proposal covers websites and platform management only. That changes the shape of the decision. What matters is the quality of the build, whether your partners can work in the platform without friction, and what it costs to bring the next property online. Those three things are what this document answers.

Attribution

Resi Pixel: source data you can verify

Pixel does not guess at lead source. It reads the evidence actually present on a visit and resolves it through rules you control.

Step 1
Pixel sees the evidence

UTM values, click IDs, referrer, page path, or an external source field carried in with the visit.

Step 2
Rules resolve it

Your rules decide which source wins, so a Google paid click can be matched on UTM source and medium together.

Step 3
The source is applied

It sets the reporting category and the exact source value written into your PMS or CRM lead source field.

Search and AI readiness

Built to be parsed, and cited

AI search can only cite a page it reads cleanly. That foundation is included on either path. The ongoing content and keyword work that turns it into citations sits with Adawk under this scope, and we would rather be in the room early on copy and page architecture than retrofit it later.

Transition

No gap in lead flow at cutover

Switching providers is where leads get lost, and Charles Street has been through that before. Every site is validated end to end before its domain moves, not after.

1
The new site is built and tested on a staging URL
Your current site stays live and untouched while the replacement is built, populated, and reviewed.
2
Integrations and form routing are verified before cutover
PMS sync, form submissions, and lead routing are tested against live systems so a submitted form lands where it should from the first minute.
3
Attribution is confirmed with the tester
Source resolution, the outbound lead source value, and hidden form fields are checked per property before the site is relied on, not diagnosed afterward.
4
301 redirects map the old site to the new one
Existing URLs are mapped so search equity and live ad links carry over instead of breaking on launch day.
5
Domain transition, then monitoring
The domain moves once everything above passes. Pricing, availability, and lead generation issues carry the highest support priority in our system, so anything that surfaces post-launch goes to the front of the queue.

Questions the team will ask

Answers to keep handy while socializing this internally.

Partner access and ownership

How exactly does Adawk get access?

We invite them as a client admin on your account. That gives them CMS access to work on the sites directly alongside your team, with no ticket queue in between. Access is granted and revoked by you, so if the agency relationship changes, the access changes with it.

Who owns the Google properties and ad accounts?

Charles Street does, from day one. Google Business Profile, Analytics, Ads, and Search Console are client-owned. Any listing or ad account Adawk builds on your behalf belongs to you, not to Resi and not to the agency. Nothing has to be reclaimed later.

What about Google Tag Manager and hosting credentials?

Tag Manager runs through a parallel container, so Adawk can deploy and manage their own tags without touching the container that drives platform functionality. Hosting and SFTP credentials are not issued to third parties. That is a platform-wide security position, not a Charles Street exception, and it is the reason the uptime and backup commitments in this proposal hold.

Platform and build

Does Resi integrate with Entrata and Elise, and what if the CRM changes?

Both are established integrations for us. Resi is integration-agnostic by design, with 42+ native connections across PMS, CRM, ILS, listing services, and analytics, and you get visibility into sync logs and control over sync frequency. If the CRM changes mid-term, we reconnect it on the backend and the sites keep running. Nothing gets rebuilt. We confirm your live connections property by property during discovery rather than assuming them.

How do we know the lead tracking is actually working this time?

Because you can check it before you depend on it. The Pixel tester previews how a given URL, referrer, or click ID resolves, and shows the source, the value that will be written into your lead source field, and the hidden form fields, per property, before the site goes live. After launch, the observation log records the raw evidence behind every visit, so a number in a report can always be traced back to why it resolved that way. Unmatched evidence gets grouped into Discovered so new traffic becomes a rule instead of falling into Other.

Are the sites built for AI search?

Every site is delivered search-ready: schema markup and structured data, meta titles and descriptions, image alt tags, GA4 and Tag Manager, and a Google Search Console connection, on clean semantic page structure. An AI crawler can only cite a page it parses cleanly, and that is what the foundation is for. The ongoing content strategy on top of it sits with Adawk under this scope.

What are your support response times?

24 to 48 hours on standard tickets, with emergency protocols outside business hours. Tickets are triaged by priority, and anything touching pricing, availability, or lead generation goes to the top of the queue automatically. Web support sits with a dedicated client experience function alongside the team that built your sites, so requests do not get handed between groups.

How do you handle a property with two buildings on one plot?

Sawyer & Huck runs as a single site with segmented availability, so a renter shopping one building sees that building's floor plans and pricing without leaving the site. It stays one domain, one CMS entry, and one management line rather than two.

What can our team do without a dev ticket?

Post-launch your team has full builder access with 500+ layouts and 92+ elements. You can build landing pages, swap sections, and spin up microsites directly. On the Launch tier you can also submit tickets for content and photo updates if you would rather our team handle them.

When the next property comes online, what's the turnaround?

On the Enterprise Theme, 3 to 5 business days per site after asset collection. Asset collection typically takes 1 to 2 days, and our CX team pre-fills the onboarding form by scraping existing sites and flagging content gaps. Your contracted rollout rate applies to every property added during the term.

Investment and buy-in

What is the simplest way to frame the total investment?

Two recurring numbers and one fixed one. Website management is $284.05 per site per month, identical on either path. Adding a property later costs $2,337.50 on the Enterprise path. Everything else is a one-time build cost, and on the Enterprise path that build is reused by every property the portfolio adds from here.

Why Enterprise if it costs more up front?

It costs $9,300 more to start and $1,225 less for every property added after launch. At the nine additional properties Charles Street expects, Enterprise is the lower total investment by $1,725, and the gap keeps widening. You also end up owning a framework built for your brand rather than a set of customized templates.

Is SEO or digital advertising part of this?

No. This proposal is websites and platform management only, since Adawk holds SEO and paid media. Both are available from Resi if that changes, and would be scoped separately.

What happens at the end of the term?

Discounted rates are tied to a 3-year agreement. Your contracted rollout rate holds for every property added during that term, which is what makes the growth math above work without a renegotiation each time a property comes online.
